About Me

I am Lucas Deus Ishigita, a graduate of the University of Dar es Salaam with a Bachelor of Arts with Education, specialising in Geography and Literature (Class of 2026). My teaching experience has equipped me with practical skills in lesson planning, classroom management, learner assessment, and the implementation of Tanzania's Competence-Based Curriculum (CBC).

I believe that education should inspire curiosity, critical thinking, and lifelong learning. My teaching approach encourages active learner participation, collaborative learning, and the integration of real-world examples into classroom instruction. I am committed to creating an inclusive learning environment where every student has the opportunity to develop academically, socially, and personally.

Beyond teaching, I have developed curriculum materials, assessment tools, and academic projects that strengthen both instructional quality and learner achievement. I continuously seek opportunities to improve my professional knowledge through educational research, collaboration, and reflective teaching practice.

Career Objective

To build a professional career as a secondary school educator dedicated to improving learning outcomes through innovative teaching, curriculum development, educational research, and continuous professional growth while contributing positively to the advancement of quality education.

02

Teaching Philosophy

"Effective teaching extends beyond delivering content — it guides learners to explore ideas, ask meaningful questions, and apply knowledge to solve real-life problems."

My classroom emphasises learner-centred instruction, critical thinking, collaboration, creativity, and continuous assessment in line with Tanzania's Competence-Based Curriculum. I strive to cultivate an environment where learners feel respected, motivated, and confident to achieve their academic potential.
